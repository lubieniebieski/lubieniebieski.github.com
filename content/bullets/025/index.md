Welcome to Bullets #25! Sit back, relax, and dive into the latest edition of the [series][2]. You can also catch up on the previous edition [bullets/24][1].

---

- Recently my brain almost exploded trying to deal with a few big features, multiple stacked PRs, unanswered questions from the business, dozens of my team's PRs waiting for review + multiple interruptions outside of the computer. It was a lot. I definitely hit the limits of my brain capacity. It wasn't a nice feeling! I still have to understand what's going on behind the code I deliver. I wonder how long we're going to keep doing that. Will there be a point in time when we say that we have enough confidence in our harnesses and just guiding the agent is going to be enough?
- On the other hand, for my pet projects like this blog, I was finally able to implement a light/dark switcher, new post types, etc. It's not about the number of visits I get (that didn't change, still millions of users every day), but it feels nice that I can improve my little site without spending time on bugs or new stuff that I'm totally unfamiliar with. Right now, I can spend the time I save on other things, like exploring Night City or discovering new islands in Anno 1800 🤷🏻‍♂️
- BTW, I'm giving social media another go. I migrated my Mastodon account from [social.lol][6] to [@lubieniebieski@mastodon.social][7]. I still love what [Adam][3] is doing there, but unfortunately I haven't used the platform enough and decided to move to a free instance instead.
- Apart from the Mastodon migration, I decided that my content is going to be created here on this blog and syndicated elsewhere. It's very hard for me to be active in multiple communities, but at the same time, I'd like to stay in touch and let other people know what I'm doing. Another thing is that I feel bad about just reading and not contributing... I found the right model for me in what [Justin Searls][4] came up with. I even started using [POSSE Party][5] to handle the syndication. Let's see how it goes - as long as I stay active and post something from time to time, we should be good.
- Autumn is in full swing. I start work when it's grey outside and finish when it's already dark. It's freezing outside, mud is all around the place and my favourite running tracks have turned into monster truck tracks. Not a fan of this kind of weather, maybe it's a good enough reason to buy GTA VI and ignore what's going on outside?
- On the positive side, I'm rewatching Ted Lasso, this time with my wife, and it's super nice to share these moments with her. We need this type of happiness on a daily basis! Believe!

That's it for this edition! Stay safe and wear some reflective clothing after dark! ✌️
