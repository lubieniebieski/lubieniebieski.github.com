- I'll start with the fact that "24" is my favorite number!
- Finished the renovation of the bathroom, I'm super happy that we have one more room with furniture built by myself! Everything works, even the automation of the various lights and a fan seems to function perfectly! Shelly + Home Assistant ftw!
- Also! My home office setup has changed! Put some tiles on the floor, decorated one wall with felt, added some lighting, lost the battle with cable management under the desk but overall, it's a nice update, definitely worth upgrading the [Workspace entry][3]! As always, soon!
- Note to self: what's the point of writing about "in progress" stuff and announcing some future updates instead of actually finishing them first and then sharing the results?

I went all in with AI in my side projects, because I prefer to make some progress during the limited time I have rather than struggling with the tasks by keeping the code human-only.
